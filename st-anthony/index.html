Our story

A Thane bakery, eight decades in the making.

St. Anthony's has been baking on Kolbad Road since 1940. We keep to traditional recipes and quality ingredients, and our experienced bakers take pride in their craft, so each bake is made with care and attention to detail.

Whether you're after a sweet indulgence or a savoury snack, there's a seat waiting for you. We're committed to serving the local community with baked goods that reflect our love for the art of baking, and in our kitchen we cook authentic Goan food.
