Faith formation · teacher guide

Adapt your teaching to the age group

The same Gospel, met at each stage of growth: from discovering Jesus through play, to exploring and participating, to questioning and connecting, to reflecting, discerning and living the faith.

Classes 1–2

Discover Jesus

Short activities, concrete objects, movement and repetition.

  • Use picture cards, puppets, songs, action rhymes and colouring.
  • Teach one main message at a time.
  • Use simple prayer gestures and familiar examples from home.

End with“What can I do this week to show Jesus’ love?”

Classes 3–5

Explore and participate

Stories, puzzles, teamwork and practical applications.

  • Organise Bible-story sequencing and Scripture treasure hunts.
  • Use quizzes, role-play, drawing and group challenges.
  • Introduce the meaning behind prayers, symbols and Church celebrations.

ConnectAsk students to connect a Bible story to a situation in school or at home.

Classes 6–8

Question and connect

Problem-solving, discussion, reflection and collaboration.

  • Use moral dilemmas, case studies and Bible investigations.
  • Let students prepare short skits, podcasts, posters or presentations.
  • Connect the Ten Commandments and Jesus’ teachings to friendships, honesty, peer pressure and responsibility.

InviteInvite questions about faith and allow respectful discussion.

Classes 9–11

Reflect, discern and live the faith

Authentic dialogue, critical thinking, discernment and service.

  • Begin with a real-life question rather than a lecture.
  • Explore Scripture in context and connect it to Catholic teaching.
  • Discuss conscience, vocation, suffering, justice, relationships, digital behaviour and social responsibility.
  • Use debates, reflective journals, service projects and student-led prayer.

EncourageEncourage students to examine why they believe, not merely repeat what they have learnt.
